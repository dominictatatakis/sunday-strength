Privacy

This describes what the service does today. Have it reviewed before launch.

What we keep

Your email address; your training preferences (days per week, experience, equipment, whether to include a run); a scrambled hash of your password if you set one, never the password itself; the sets, reps and weights you log; bodyweights you choose to record; which weekly emails were sent; and your subscription status.

Signing in with Apple or Google

If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive and keep the account identifier they give us and the email address they share — which, with Apple's Hide My Email, is a private relay address. We never receive your Apple or Google password.

Who handles it

The app runs on Render. Your data is stored in a Supabase Postgres database in London. Plan emails are sent through Brevo. If payments are switched on, Stripe handles billing and card details, which we never see. Apple and Google handle their own sign-in.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking cookies. The only cookie keeps you signed in.

Leaving

Cancel any time from your account or the link in any plan email. To have your account and everything logged against it deleted, reply to any plan email and ask.
